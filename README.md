# book-orders-sql-analysis
SQL Analysis of books orders and sales covering different aspects of book sales to establish a relationship between books and customers 
various key insights can be drawn from this analysis so that which will help the shops to generate more revenue such as - which author's book is sold the most , which book is most expensive , how many books are there in particular genre and which genre is preferred by the consumers , average price of the book etc ..

this analysis answers business questions such as :- 

1.customers who ordered more than 1 quantity of a book 
2.all orders where amount exceeded $20
3.books with lowest stock 
4.total revenue generated from all orders 
5.total books sold for each genre 
etc .. 

key insights :-
we can conclude that this analysis is helpful in establishing a business relationship between customers and the order such as Q1 will help us determine customers who are potentially buying more books , Q2 will help us differentiate customers on basis of income , Q3 will alert us to avail the stock for books which will be soon out of stock , Q4 helps us in telling whether the overall reneue was adequate or not , Q5 will tell whether which genre is profitable and worth selling for ..
